DELIBE-325: Add the problem reporting feature: an `imio.reportproblem.reportable` marker behavior, a `@@report-problem` form offered either in a modal or as a full page, a `report-problem-button` viewlet, and a site-wide control panel for the recipients, the reasons, the wording and the display mode. Two extension points let a consumer package decide who receives a report (`IProblemReportConfig`) and what happens once it is sent (`IProblemReportedEvent`); the add-on itself persists nothing. hCaptcha protection and `collective.fingerpointing` audit logging are optional extras — without the captcha the form is deliberately not offered to anonymous visitors, and the audit line carries no personal data about the reporter.
